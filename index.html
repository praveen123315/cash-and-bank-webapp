Cash Payroll

Department-wise totals

Select the month first. Attendance and advance files are saved only for the month you select here.

1 · Machine attendance for

Choose the file exported from the attendance machine (ATT_MOTHLY.xls). The app reads the monthly totals: present paid, holiday, leave, off days, OT hours, fooding and late marks.

2 · Advance Excel for

Upload the advance list you make in Excel (columns like Emp ID, Name and the amount). Each row is matched to the Master by Emp ID (or old code), otherwise by name. The amounts are saved for the month selected at the top and show in Monthly Inputs, Payroll and the Advance page. If an employee already has an advance saved for this month, the amount is updated — never added twice.

3 · Employee Master from wages sheet

Choose your wages workbook (like WAGES_SEP_2026.xlsx). It needs the ALL tab; the WAGES tab gives Form 12 Salary. Do this once, and again when employees join, leave or get increments. It replaces the whole Master — after the first import, add and edit employees on the Master page instead.

Monthly inputs —

Boxes show this month's machine figures, suggested Form 12 days and the monthly incentive (₹500 when no day is short in the month, ₹300 when 1 day short — leave counts as a day short). Change any box, then Save & calculate. Advances are entered on the Advance page.

Employee Master

Changes are saved straight to the Master tab in the Google Sheet. Set Status to Inactive when someone leaves — don't delete them, old months still use their record.

Advance history

Every saved advance, by month. An advance saved for a month (for example on 22 October for October 2026) is deducted when that month's attendance is processed, and shows in Monthly Inputs and Payroll.
